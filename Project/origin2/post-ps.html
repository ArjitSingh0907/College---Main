FOUNDATIONAL CONTEXT

What Is a Hackathon?

A hackathon is an intensive, sprint-like technology competition where cross-functional teams of software developers, hardware engineers, data scientists, and UI/UX designers collaborate continuously under time constraints. Instead of theoretical pitch decks or slide presentations, participants are evaluated entirely on demonstrable, working software and physical prototypes built from scratch.

⏱️ The 36-Hour Sprint

Teams code, assemble circuits, and debug non-stop in an on-campus arena, transforming conceptual architectures into working products.

🧩 Authentic Challenges

Students do not work on textbook exercises. They build custom solutions to unreleased business and technical problems provided by external organizations.

⚖️ Rigorous Evaluation

Prototypes are judged by senior industry practitioners on codebase stability, architectural viability, scalability, and UX design.

THE FLAGSHIP INITIATIVE

What Is ORIGIN '26 & How Is It Conducted?

ORIGIN '26 is the flagship national hackathon organized by Techniki at Amity School of Engineering & Technology (ASET), Amity University Haryana. It brings together hundreds of top-tier undergraduate engineering students across North India for an uninterrupted 36-hour physical build marathon on campus.

1. Companies Define the Tasks

Your brand or organization submits between 1 and 5 bottleneck challenges or technical wish-list features through the form below.

2. Architecture Review

Prior to entering the physical arena, applicant teams submit a two-page system blueprint. Only cohorts with solid engineering proposals are admitted.

3. Direct IP & Hiring Access

Partnering brands obtain functional prototypes, review open-source or proprietary solutions, and evaluate top talent for high-impact internship or engineering roles.
